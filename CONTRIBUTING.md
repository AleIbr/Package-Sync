## Branches

Currently, Package-Sync follows the [git-flow branching model](http://nvie.com/posts/a-successful-git-branching-model/) in a way. This means that there are two main branches, master and develop. Master usually only points to the current stable release version. Most people use the develop branch to get hands-on latest features. On release, the stable develop version is merged into the Master branch.

All release versions will also be available as branches.

## Pull Request

To fix a problem in Package-Sync or to contribute to Package-Sync you can fork the Package-Sync project on GitHub. This will generate a personal cloned repository for you to work on. To reincorporate your changes into Package-Sync after they have been completed and tested, just send us a [pull request](https://help.github.com/articles/using-pull-requests). This will allow us to review your changes and merge them into the develop branch. In your pull request, please include:
1. A description of the changes you made
2. Tests regarding your changes (if possible)

### Style guidelines

There are no style guidelines at the moment. Just try to keep everything clean.
